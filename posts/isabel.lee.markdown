### Who are you, and what do you do?

I'm [Isabel](https://isabisabel.com/ "Isabel's website.")! I'm a game developer, artist, and writer based in NYC. I make web games like [Number Gacha](https://isabisabel.com/gacha/ "A web-based number-themed gacha game.") and [Breadwinner](https://breadwinner.app/ "A web-based bread-themed clicker game.") that deconstruct game mechanics and interrogate why we play. I also sometimes [write](https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/lee_03_21/ "A sci-fi story by Isabel about Fibonacci.") [stories](https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/lee_09_20/ "A sci-fi story by Isabel about a machine that simulates the universe."). I love exploring the intersection between games, storytelling, and psychology.

### What do you use to get the job done?

I use a M3 [Macbook Pro][macbook-pro] for development. Usually it's paired with a [huge, curved monitor][s3425dw], which I got from my job a few years ago and now can't go back-- I love seeing all of my windows on one cohesive view. I also use the old-school [wired Apple earbuds][earpods] for sound. It's lightweight, and a tangible mic is so much easier to handle for meetings. I have a pre-built 80% [mechanical keyboard][k8] (I once built my own, but it was bad and died-- I've learned my lesson) with a well-loved fidget spinner keycap in the corner. 

I use [Figma][] to sketch out my ideas, and [React][] + [VSCode][visual-studio-code] to implement them. I rotate between other tools depending on the project's needs-- Claude, [Notion][], and [GIMP][] are common supplements. My most used web tools are probably remove.bg and [ezgif.com][].

I have an [iPad Air Max][ipad-air] that I use for digital art. I use [Procreate][procreate-ios] and an [Apple Pencil][pencil] to get the job done there. I also like to keep a physical notebook around my setup, my preference is an unlined original Moleskine. If I'm really struggling with a problem, I find that scribbling about it is the best way to unblock myself.

### What would be your dream setup?

I would love what I currently have, and then a space next to it that I could use as a painting studio. My cables would all be managed AMAZINGLY. And then maybe I'd have a nice view beyond my monitor, of a city skyline or body of water or both.
