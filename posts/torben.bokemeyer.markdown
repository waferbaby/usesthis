### Who are you, and what do you do?

Hi! I'm Torben or [Turbo Torbo](https://linktr.ee/TurboTorbo "Torben's links.") on all platforms. I'm a [freelance illustrator](https://turbotorbo.me/ "Torben's website.") from Berlin, Germany, and I draw things for games and anything playful.

In the past I did artworks for various TTRPGs ([Notorious](https://alwayscheckers.com/products/notorious-zine "A tabletop RPG about bounty hunters taking on contracts."), [Last Sentinels](https://alwayscheckers.com/products/last-sentinels-zine "A tabletop RPG about defending a starship being persued through space."), [Orbital](https://mouseholepress.itch.io/orbital "A tabletop RPG about running a space station during interstellar war."), among others), boardgames ([Merchants of Andromeda](https://www.allplay.com/board-games/merchants-of-andromeda "A board game dealing with space politics."), [Lost & Found Collection](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/jhrrsn/the-lost-and-found-collection/ "A collection of card games about objects having a will of their own."), [Notorious Card Game](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/alwayscheckers/notorious-tales-of-hardscrabble-bounty-hunting "A card version of the bounty hunter tabletop RPG.")), video games ([Space Sprouts][space-sprouts], various serious games) and I've been involved in creating and maintaining the yearly key art for anime & gaming convention [POLARIS](https://polaris-con.de/ "A gaming and anime convention in Hamburg, Germany.") in Hamburg. Whenever I get the chance I also design funky user interfaces like [this app](https://turbotorbo.me/commercial/polaris-con#app "Torben's designs for a companion app for the POLARIS conference.") or [this browser MMO UI](https://turbotorbo.me/commercial/indie-arena-booth-online-2020#userinterface "Torben's designs for the interface for an online game conference.").

I'm also co-founder of the teeny-tiny indie game studio [Schleuder Games](https://schleuder.games/ "Torben's gaming studio.").

### What do you use to get the job done?

I work on a [Huion Kamvas Pro 24 4K][kamvas-pro-24] with a no-name ultrawide monitor as a second screen. When I'm not working from home I use an [OMEN 16 notebook][omen-16-max] with a [Huion Kamvas Pro 16 2.5K][kamvas-pro-16]. Honourable mention: My [Logitech MX Vertical mouse][mx-vertical], it really helped me get rid of wrist pain.

On the software side I draw in [Clip Studio Paint Pro][clip-studio-paint] and use [Lazy Nezumi Pro][lazy-nezumi-pro] as a ruler extension that helpes me out so so much. I got my own library of custom brushes but what I find myself using really often are the [Micron brushes by Halftone Hospital][real-microns] (Space Sprouts for example was drawn completely with this). For final drawing, layout & export I use the [Adobe Suite][creative-suite], but I'm currently trying to get more used to [Affinity][affinity-photo]. I also use [Miro][] extensively - for moodboards, project management, client feedback, whatever. Most of my direct communication is handled via [Discord][], for emails, document editing and cloud storage I use Google products.

I also do lots of traditional drawing. Currently I'm working my way through a dotted Rhodia Goalbook, but I'm always on the lookout for new sketchbooks that I like. Most of the time I use [Muji Ink ballpoint pens][gel-ink-ballpoint], [Pentel Sign pens][sign-pen] and [Pigma Micron fineliners][pigma-micron]. Posca Markers and Arrtx color markers when I feel colorful. And lastly a small shoutout to Pentel Pocket Brush, my beloved.

### What would be your dream setup?

I'm good on the technical side. I would love a bigger dedicated space for drawing though. A nice space with lots of natural light. A big old table for drawing, cutting, binding. Big drawers for properly storing artworks and prints. Nice large bookshelves filled with rad art books and comics. And a sweet comfy chair to have a coffee break and read (and then nap for a bit).
